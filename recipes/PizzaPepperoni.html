Pizza Pepperoni Recipe

Home Page

Description :

Pizza Pepperoni is a one of the most famous foods in the planet, it looks like uhhhhhh sooo delicious.

Ingredients :

  • Pizza dough - the base of the pizza.
  • Tomato sauce and mozzarella cheese add the main flavor and texture.
  • Pepperoni - the main topping that gives the pizza its spicy, savory flavor.
  • Olive oil and Italian herbs - add extra flavor.

Steps :

  1. Prepare the dough - Roll the pizza dough into a round shape and place it on a baking tray.
  2. Add the sauce - Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  3. Add the toppings - Sprinkle mozzarella cheese and place pepperoni slices on top.
  4. Bake the pizza - Bake it in a preheated oven until the cheese melts and the crust becomes golden.
  5. Serve - Let the pizza cool for a few minutes, then slice and serve.
