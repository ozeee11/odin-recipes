Shawarma Recipe

Home Page

Description

Shawarma is a delicious food in the Middle East, especially in Syria and Iraq.

Ingredients

  • Chicken or beef the main meat used for shawarma.
  • Shawarma spices a mixture of spices that gives the meat its distinctive flavor.
  • Yogurt, lemon juice, and garlic used to marinate and add flavor to the meat.
  • Olive oil, salt, and black pepper used to season the meat.
  • Flatbread, vegetables, pickles, and garlic sauce used to serve the shawarma.

Steps :

  1. Marinate the meat - Mix the meat with yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, spices, salt, and pepper.
  2. Cook the meat - Cook the marinated meat until it is fully cooked and lightly browned.
  3. Prepare the bread - Place the cooked meat inside the flatbread.
  4. Add the toppings - Add vegetables, pickles, and garlic sauce.
  5. Roll and serve - Roll the shawarma tightly and serve it while warm.
